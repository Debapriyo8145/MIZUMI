MIZUMI AQUATICS

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect when you use the MIZUMI Aquatics website and how we use it.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors, customers and account holders using the MIZUMI Aquatics website. MIZUMI Aquatics is based in Ranaghat, Nadia, West Bengal, India. For privacy questions, please use our Contact page.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, phone number and, where provided, email address.
  • Delivery details: address, locality, city, state, PIN code and country.
  • Order details: products ordered, quantities, prices, order notes, order status and payment status.
  • Account details: information you provide when registering, signing in or updating your customer profile and saved addresses.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated when you use the website, such as browser, device, server and security logs.

Please do not submit information that is not needed to place or manage your order.

3. How we use information

We use information for purposes such as:

  • processing, confirming, packing and delivering orders;
  • contacting you about an order, delivery, DOA claim or customer-support request;
  • maintaining customer accounts, order history and saved addresses;
  • preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorised access and duplicate or invalid orders;
  • maintaining, troubleshooting and improving website operations; and
  • meeting applicable legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The website may use browser storage, such as local storage, to remember shopping-cart contents or support website functionality. Your browser may also store technical information needed for account sessions or security. You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings, but some website features may then stop working correctly.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share only the information reasonably needed with service providers who help us operate the website, host systems, communicate about orders, process payments if an online payment option is introduced, or deliver products. We may also disclose information where required by law, a valid legal process, or to protect our rights, customers or systems.

We do not promise that every service provider or technology used by the website is located in India. Where information is processed by a third-party provider, that provider may handle it under its own terms and privacy practices, subject to applicable law.

6. Data security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect customer information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No website, internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. How long we keep information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, manage customer accounts and orders, resolve disputes, maintain business records and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Our website is intended for people who can lawfully make purchases. A parent or lawful guardian should make purchases on behalf of a child where required by applicable law. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, business processes or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will be published on this page.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, visit our Contact page and mention that your query concerns privacy or personal information.
