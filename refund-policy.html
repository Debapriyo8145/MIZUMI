MIZUMI AQUATICS

Return & Refund Policy

Please review this policy carefully before ordering live fish, aquarium products or accessories.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. General policy

MIZUMI Aquatics does not accept general returns or provide refunds for change of mind, incorrect selection, ordering the wrong item or a change in personal preference. This policy does not exclude any consumer right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

2. Live fish cancellation

  • A customer may request cancellation of a live fish order only while its status is Pending and before MIZUMI confirms the order.
  • After MIZUMI confirms the order, customer-requested cancellation is not available.
  • If MIZUMI cannot fulfil an order, we will contact the customer and handle any payment received in accordance with applicable law.

3. Dead on Arrival (DOA) — live fish

DOA reporting deadline: within 1 hour of delivery. A customer who receives a live fish that is dead on arrival may request a refund by reporting the issue within one hour and providing video proof.

To submit a DOA claim:

  1. Check the fish promptly when the delivery arrives.
  2. Contact MIZUMI within 1 hour of delivery. Include your order number and the time the delivery was received.
  3. Provide clear video proof showing the affected fish and its condition on arrival. Keep the packaging and relevant evidence until the claim has been reviewed.
  4. Follow any reasonable instructions from MIZUMI if additional information is needed to assess the claim.

DOA refunds are subject to verification. If the claim is approved, MIZUMI will arrange the refund through the original payment method where possible, or agree on an appropriate refund method with the customer. The refund decision and amount will be communicated to the customer.

4. Wrong, damaged or defective products

If you receive an item different from what you ordered, or an item arrives damaged or defective, contact MIZUMI as soon as reasonably possible. Provide your order number, a description of the issue and clear photographs or video. We will review the circumstances and determine an appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable law. Do not discard the item or packaging until we respond, where it is reasonable to retain them.

5. Other refund requests

Requests outside the situations described above are not accepted as a general policy. Exceptional circumstances may be reviewed individually. Nothing in this policy limits any refund, replacement or other remedy that MIZUMI is required to provide under applicable consumer protection law.

6. How to report an issue

Use the Contact page to report a DOA, wrong, damaged or defective item. Include your order number, delivery time and supporting evidence. For DOA claims, the one-hour reporting deadline applies.

7. Refund processing

Once a refund is approved, MIZUMI will initiate it using the original payment method where possible. For Cash on Delivery orders, MIZUMI will contact the customer to arrange an appropriate refund method. The time taken for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or bank.

8. Policy updates

MIZUMI may update this policy to reflect changes in business operations or legal requirements. The latest version and update date will be displayed on this page. Any update remains subject to applicable law and does not remove rights that have already accrued to a customer.
